About Patrick C. Washington

As a 23-year retired military veteran, I spent over two decades trained to lead, protect, and look after service members and their families. Having packed up and relocated my own family multiple times throughout my military career, I know firsthand the real stress, tight timelines, and heavy lift of a major relocation or PCS move. I bring that exact same duty, discipline, and straightforward communication to every real estate client I serve.

Whether you're right here in Hardin County or looking to move to Kentucky from across the country, buying or selling real estate shouldn't be a game of hide-and-seek with the truth. I bridge the gap for out-of-town clients—having successfully managed and closed complex, 100+ acre property transactions entirely remote—ensuring a seamless experience from start to finish.
